Privacy Policy

App: ALFRED-IPTV · Developer: ALFRED-TECH · Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how the ALFRED-IPTV app ("the app") handles your information.

Information we collect

ALFRED-TECH does not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no user accounts with us, no analytics and no advertising.

Information stored on your device

The playlist details you enter (server address, username and password, or M3U link), your favorites, watch history and settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to ALFRED-TECH.

Connections the app makes

To play content, the app connects directly to the server of the playlist provider you entered. That provider may receive information such as your IP address, as with any internet connection. Their handling of your data is covered by their own privacy policy.

Permissions

Internet and network state: to load your playlist and play streams. No location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access is requested.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. Parents can use Parental Control to lock categories.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app stores by removing your playlists in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
